# Editorial Standards Draft

NXTSmart.Homes publishes smart home guides, product comparisons, reviews, and setup resources for homeowners planning connected devices, automation, security, energy, entertainment, and whole-home integrations.

## How topics are selected

Topics are selected based on reader usefulness, smart home setup complexity, product-category demand, and whether the article can help a homeowner make a clearer decision. The site may also cover product categories that include affiliate links.

## Product evaluation

Product coverage may consider setup experience, compatibility, useful smart-home features and automation, privacy and security considerations, reliability signals available from product information, and overall value. NXTSmart.Homes does not claim hands-on lab testing unless a specific article clearly states that hands-on testing was performed.

## Scoring methodology

When product scores appear, they should be based on a documented editorial scoring model. The approved scoring factors are: features and automation, compatibility and ecosystem fit, setup and ease of use, privacy and security, reliability and support signals, and value. Scores should not be presented as reader-submitted ratings.

## Affiliate relationship

NXTSmart.Homes may earn a commission when readers buy through affiliate links. Affiliate relationships do not add cost for readers. Affiliate disclosure should remain visible on monetized pages.

## Corrections

Readers can request corrections through the contact page. Corrections should be reviewed against available sources and updated when a factual error is confirmed.
